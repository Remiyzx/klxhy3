Chinese Number Culture

4
UNLUCKY
sì

Sounds like "death" (sǐ). In China and some Malaysian Chinese areas, people skip 4 and 14 on car plates and elevator buttons.

6
LUCKY
liù

Sounds like "smooth" (liù). Represents good fortune. "666" is very popular on car plates and phone numbers!

8
WEALTH
bā

Sounds like "prosper" (fā). Many people say "Gōngxǐ fācái" during CNY, meaning "Wishing you prosperity!"

How to Say "2" in Chinese?

2
èr
  • Use "èr" when referring to the number itself.
  • Counting: yī, èr, sān...
  • Ordinal numbers: èr céng (second floor).
2
liǎng
  • Use "liǎng" when indicating a specific quantity that requires a measure word.
  • Example: liǎng ge rén (two people). "ge" is the measure word!

4. Hundreds & Zeros Rules KLXHY pg 15

Hundreds: 100, 200...

100: yìbǎi
200: èrbǎi OR liǎngbǎi

  • èrbǎi is often used in more formal contexts or when numbers are being counted in sequence.
  • liǎngbǎi is more natural when talking about having 200 of something.
Rule 1: The "líng" Rule (x0x)

When saying numbers in the hundreds where the tens digit is 0, the word líng (zero) MUST be pronounced!

101 = yìbǎi líng yī
105 = yìbǎi líng wǔ
602 = liùbǎi líng èr

Pattern Example (76,809):
qī wàn liù qiān bā bǎi líng jiǔ

Rule 2: The "yī" Rule (x1x)

When saying numbers in the hundreds where the tens digit is 1, the word yī (one) MUST be pronounced!

111 = yìbǎi yīshíyī
117 = yìbǎi yīshíqī
913 = jiǔbǎi yīshísān

Pattern Example (76,819):
qī wàn liù qiān bā bǎi yīshí jiǔ

Hundreds + Tens (Normal)

250 liǎngbǎi wǔshí
370 sānbǎi qīshí
490 sìbǎi jiǔshí

9. Wrap-up & Homework

Core Points Summary:

  • 0-10 & Culture: Remember 4 is unlucky (sǐ), 8 is lucky (fā)!
  • The Two 2s: "èr" for numbers/math. "liǎng" for quantity/time.
  • Hundreds Rules: x0x (e.g., 105) uses "líng". x1x (e.g., 115) uses "yī".
  • Currency: kuài, máo, fēn. (RM 76.84 = qīshíliù kuài bā máo sì fēn).
  • Time: diǎn, fēn. (2:00 = liǎng diǎn).

Homework Task:

2. Practice telling today's date and time in Pinyin out loud.
